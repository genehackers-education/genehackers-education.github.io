About

Speaker series

Alongside the classroom visits, we bring in researchers and founders from across synthetic biology — CRISPR, protein design, biomanufacturing, cell therapies, and more — to talk about their work and how they got into it.

It's a chance for students to meet the people behind the field and ask them anything. Here's everyone who's joined us so far, most recent first.
